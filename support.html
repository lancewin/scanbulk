Subscription Management

  • Subscriptions auto-renew and can be cancelled anytime
  • Manage in "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions"

Team Collaboration

  • Requires membership
  • Create a collaboration and send the invite link to your teammate
  • Every iPhone keeps the same history in real time

iCloud Backup

  • Requires membership
  • Manual backup to your own iCloud, restore on a new iPhone

FAQ

Is my data safe?
By default everything stays on your device. If you turn on iCloud Backup or Team Collaboration, data is stored in iCloud and synced through Apple's servers — the developer cannot read it.
How do I cancel my subscription?
Go to "Settings → Apple ID → Subscriptions" on your phone, find ScanBulk, and select cancel.
How do I restore membership on a new device?
Open the app on your new device, go to "Settings → Restore Purchases".
