ScanBulk Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, upload, or analyze any of your personal data. Your data belongs only to you and lives only on your device and in your own iCloud.

Privacy Commitments

  • No registration or login — No account needed
  • No server — We do not operate servers to receive data
  • No personal data collection — No name/phone/location
  • No data upload — Data stays on your device and in your iCloud; the developer operates no server to receive it
  • No third-party tracking — No ads/analytics SDK
  • No ads — Zero ad monetization

Permission Usage

  • Camera: Scan QR codes and barcodes; frames are processed locally only, never uploaded
  • Photo Library (Read): Recognize codes from album images, never uploaded
  • Photo Library (Write): Save generated code images to album

Subscriptions & Payments

  • Payments are processed by Apple App Store; we never touch payment information
  • Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID; no separate account system

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device by default (SwiftData/UserDefaults)
  • If you enable iCloud Backup, backup files are stored in your own private iCloud Drive space, accessible only with your Apple ID — the developer cannot read them
  • iCloud Backup is off by default and can be turned off at any time in Settings → iCloud Backup, where you can also delete the uploaded files
  • Uninstalling the app clears all local data (backups in iCloud must be deleted manually in Settings)
