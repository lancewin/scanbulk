ScanBulk Privacy Policy

This app does not collect, upload, or analyze any of your personal data. Your data belongs only to you and exists only on your device.

Privacy Commitments

  • No registration or login — No account needed
  • No server — We do not operate servers to receive data
  • No personal data collection — No name/phone/location
  • No data upload — Data stays on device only
  • No third-party tracking — No ads/analytics SDK
  • No ads — Zero ad monetization

Permission Usage

  • Camera: Scan QR codes and barcodes; frames are processed locally only, never uploaded
  • Photo Library (Read): Recognize codes from album images, never uploaded
  • Photo Library (Write): Save generated code images to album

Subscriptions & Payments

  • Payments are processed by Apple App Store; we never touch payment information
  • Subscriptions are tied to your Apple ID; no separate account system

Data Storage

  • All data is stored locally on your device (SwiftData/UserDefaults)
  • Uninstalling the app clears all local data
